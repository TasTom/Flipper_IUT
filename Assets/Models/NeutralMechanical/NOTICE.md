# Neutral — mechanical assemblies

The slingshot plastic outlines are adapted from **Plastic with decal**, by Vincent
Bousquet and contributors, in [pinball-parts](https://github.com/vbousquet/pinball-parts).
Source model in this project: `Assets/Models/Parts/Miscellaneous/Plastic_with_decal.fbx`.
The outlines were measured in Unity after placement and the 8% proportion adjustment,
then rebuilt in Blender with a raised plate, bevels and original printed motifs.

`NeutralMechanical.fbx` is distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Rubber loops and the
terminal geometry were created procedurally for Vosges Mania and are distributed
under the same license. [License text](../../../Docs/NeutralValidation/2026-09-26/pinball-parts-LICENSE.md).

Metal posts and fasteners are instantiated separately from the existing
`Assets/Models/Parts/Posts/Post_-_Metal_-_114_-_Round.fbx` and
`Assets/Models/Parts/Screws/Bolt_6-32_-_Slotted_Flanged.fbx` models from the same repository.
Their existing attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 license continue to apply.

The Stern Jurassic Park service manual was used as an assembly reference.
No Stern model, artwork or decal was copied.
